# WP18 — Runtime-neutral Silero VAD gate

Date: 2026-11-26. Scope: evaluate a speech/silence gate only; **no ASR was run**.
The frozen corpus was read only. Implementation: [`apply_gate.py`](../../code/muteinous-spike50/spike/asr50/vad_gate/apply_gate.py) and [`evaluate.py`](../../code/muteinous-spike50/spike/asr50/vad_gate/evaluate.py).

## Asset and method

Installed faster-whisper 1.2.1 into disposable `C:\scratch\asr50\WP18\venv`
using binary wheels, plus SoundFile for WAV I/O. The model was copied from the
read-only WP10 faster-whisper 1.2.1 wheel asset to
`C:\scratch\asr50\WP18\silero\silero_vad_v6.onnx` (1,245,151 bytes;
SHA-256 `4cbf549b8326f60f80f2536d9eeeb450a9abe83365a098031c89719f1be17d2`).
No model weights were downloaded. The asset is package-bundled Silero VAD,
identified as MIT-licensed in the upstream package.

The thin ONNX runner uses 512-sample (32 ms) windows at 16 kHz, 64-sample
preceding context, recurrent `h`/`c` tensors shaped `(1,1,128)`, and Silero's
input/output names. The independent timestamp state machine follows
`faster_whisper.vad.get_speech_timestamps`, including threshold/hysteresis,
minimum durations and padded/merged boundaries. It matched exactly on 8
representative clips (clean, noise at 10/0/-5 dB SNR, pink noise and effects),
using threshold 0.5, minimum silence 200 ms and pad 100 ms.

## Results

Metrics use the 30 exact-boundary constructed timing clips. Speech recall is
the fraction of annotated word-interval samples covered by VAD. “Precision”
is the share of VAD speech samples within annotated word intervals expanded by
40 ms on each side (the specified gap tolerance). “Words cut” counts words
with no detected-speech overlap. Non-speech zero count uses 20 silence and
non-speech clips.

Recommended balanced setting for this synthetic set: threshold **0.5**,
neg-threshold **0.35** (default threshold minus 0.15), pad **100 ms**, minimum
speech **0 ms**, minimum silence **200 ms**. It detects speech on 19/20
non-speech/silence clips; unlike conservative higher thresholds it retains
more speech, but does not eliminate every noise false positive.

| Threshold | Pad | Min silence | Timing recall | Gap-tolerant precision | Words cut | Zero/20 non-speech |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.3 | 30 ms | 200 ms | 91.1% | 97.3% | 1 | 17 |
| 0.3 | 100 ms | 200 ms | 96.5% | 95.2% | 1 | 17 |
| 0.3 | 200 ms | 200 ms | 98.3% | 91.9% | 1 | 17 |
| 0.5 | 30 ms | 200 ms | 86.1% | 99.3% | 4 | 19 |
| 0.5 | 100 ms | 200 ms | 93.4% | 97.3% | 4 | 19 |
| 0.5 | 200 ms | 200 ms | 95.2% | 92.4% | 4 | 19 |
| 0.7 | 30 ms | 200 ms | 81.0% | 99.7% | 5 | 20 |
| 0.7 | 100 ms | 200 ms | 91.0% | 98.4% | 5 | 20 |
| 0.7 | 200 ms | 200 ms | 94.2% | 93.3% | 5 | 20 |

With threshold 0.5/pad 100 ms, changing minimum silence from 100 to 200 to
500 ms gave recall 93.25%, 93.38%, 94.45%; precision 98.04%, 97.32%, 92.73%;
words cut 4 in all cases; zero non-speech clips 19/20 in all cases. Longer
silence debounce joins separated speech and recovers a little more word audio,
but increasingly includes inter-word gaps and noise.

The only non-speech clip with a detected segment at the recommended setting
was `nonspeech_pink_noise_30s.wav`, with a short region at samples 1,472–5,184
(92–324 ms). No isolated silence or effect-only clip triggered. The full
segments for every clip, all sweep values and parity checks are in
`C:\scratch\asr50\WP18\evaluation.json`.

## Masked corpus and limitations

Produced 60 float32 mono 16 kHz WAVs (same sample count/timeline as source),
unchanged copied `ground_truth_v2.json`, and SHA-256 manifest at
`C:\scratch\asr50\WP18\corpus_v2_vadmasked\`. Settings are embedded in
`manifest.json`; every entry includes VAD segments and `gate_no_speech`.
Zero-segment clips are explicitly flagged for an ASR pipeline to skip, not
removed. `apply_gate.py` accepts any directory of 16 kHz mono WAVs and writes
timeline-preserving masked WAV copies.

Limitations:
- Timing clips are synthetic isolated-word concatenations, with artificial
  40–300 ms gaps (some 35 ms rapid gaps), not conversational coarticulated
  speech. A longer pad recovers more annotated word samples across those gaps,
  but increases speech-region contamination.
- At -5 dB SNR, the sweep's aggregate timing score includes these clips; this
  was not a separate real-world noisy-speech validation. The gate is not
  denoising: detected noise remains in the masked speech intervals and missed
  speech is irreversibly zeroed.
- Quiet -40 dBFS timing clips did receive speech segments at the recommended
  setting (three intervals each); it does not simply discard the quiet speech.
  This observation is only for the synthetic samples.
- Gate masks non-detected samples rather than deciding transcript validity.
  It cannot guarantee silence hallucination prevention; `gate_no_speech` is
  only a skip hint. Runtime-neutral means the ONNX VAD is separable from ASR,
  not that all platforms/providers have been validated.

**Summary (<250 words):** A standalone NumPy/ONNX Runtime Silero gate
reproduced faster-whisper timestamp segments exactly on 8 clips. On the 30
synthetic timing clips, the recommended threshold 0.5 / 100 ms pad / 200 ms
minimum silence covered 93.4% of exact word samples with 97.3% of gated speech
within words ±40 ms; 4 words had no overlap. It returned zero segments on
19/20 silence/non-speech clips; the remaining clip was pink noise, briefly
misclassified. Threshold 0.3/pad 200 ms raised recall to 98.3% and cut 1 word,
at the cost of 3/20 non-speech clips triggering and lower precision (91.9%).
Threshold 0.7 silenced all 20 non-speech clips but cut 5 words. All 60
timeline-preserving masked WAVs, copied ground truth and segment/hash manifest
are in WP18 scratch output. Quiet -40 dBFS synthetic speech was retained as
speech. No ASR ran; synthetic isolated-word timing and noisy-speech limits
prevent production accuracy claims. Wall-clock: approximately 15 minutes.
No blocker or maintainer approval request.
