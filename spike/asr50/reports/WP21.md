# WP21 — transcript-only lexical corpus v3

## Outcome

Created a deterministic v3 generator and README under `spike/asr50/benchmark/corpus_v3/`. The frozen delivery is `C:\scratch\asr50\corpus_v3_frozen\` (380 WAVs plus JSON ground truth and TSV clip list; every file is read-only). The content-addressed System.Speech cache and both generation runs are under `C:\scratch\asr50\WP21\`.

The corpus has 150 authored unique generic game-dialogue sentences, each rendered by Microsoft Zira and Microsoft David (300 clean clips), 40 acoustic-condition variants and 40 transcript-empty non-speech clips. Sentences cover the requested lexical phenomena, target/distractor words, numbers/dates rendered in words, contractions, names, homophones, repeats, fillers, restarts, exclamations and varied lengths. Transcript references are text-only, with no word timings. All clean lines were checked to contain 2–25 words and no digits.

Audio is mono 16 kHz IEEE float32. SAPI inputs are content-addressed by SHA-256; text, voice, rate and volume participate in the cache key. Speech clips vary SAPI rate across -4..+4 and volume 65..100. `resample_poly` performs conversion from SAPI's 22.05 kHz PCM. The generator zeroes libsndfile PEAK timestamps before final WAV hashing. Read `README.md` for transcript normalization and condition details.
