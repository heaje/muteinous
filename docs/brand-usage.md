# Muteinous brand usage

The [software license](../LICENSE) allows commercial and noncommercial use, modification and redistribution under its terms. This policy addresses branding, not software permissions, and does not add conditions to Apache-2.0.

- You may truthfully refer to Muteinous, identify compatibility or say a product is based on Muteinous, and preserve required attribution notices.
- Do not imply that an unofficial fork, modified build, service or product is an official Muteinous release, endorsed by or affiliated with the project when it is not. Clearly identify unofficial releases and their responsible distributor.
- The project does not grant general permission to use the Muteinous name or logo as branding for another product. Seek permission for uses beyond truthful reference and applicable legal rights.
- Official releases are identified by the project's [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/heaje/muteinous/releases), under the documented [release process](development.md#manual-builds-and-official-releases).

This policy does not restrict lawful nominative/fair use, required attribution, or any other independently available rights. It does not assert registered trademark status. Trademark registration is deferred; name/logo clearance has not been established.
